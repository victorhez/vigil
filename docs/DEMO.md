# Demo script (3 minutes)

Recorded on a Seeker (or any Android 11+ phone) against devnet. Screen-record the phone; narrate over it.

| Time | On screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Onboarding, first page | "Self-custody has no next of kin. When someone dies or loses their keys, their crypto goes with them." |
| 0:15 | Swipe to pages 2 and 3 | "Vigil turns your Seeker into a heartbeat. Clock in on a rhythm you choose. If you go quiet, your assets go home to the people you picked." |
| 0:30 | Connect wallet (Seed Vault approves) | "Everything that touches funds is signed by your wallet through Mobile Wallet Adapter." |
| 0:40 | Light your Vigil: 2 minutes + 1 minute grace, two heirs at 60/40, 0.1 SOL, SKR toggled on | "Heirs and shares go on-chain. Names stay on the phone. One approval creates the vault, deposits, and funds a device key." |
| 1:10 | Pulse screen, hold to clock in, fingerprint, "Clocked in. Day 1." | "That was a check-in: a hold, a fingerprint, and a transaction signed by a key sealed in the phone's hardware keystore. No wallet pop-up." |
| 1:30 | Pull down Quick Settings, tap Vigil tile; show the home screen widget | "It lives where your thumb already is: a widget, a Quick Settings tile, an app shortcut, and reminders that read the vault from the chain." |
| 1:50 | Vault tab, Protect your SKR card | "SKR is first-class. Vigil notices unprotected SKR and offers to cover it." |
| 2:05 | Switch to the heir's wallet, Legacies tab, countdown running out, pill turns "Claimable" | "Now the owner goes quiet. The heir sees the vault, the last check-in, and the countdown." |
| 2:30 | Release to all heirs, then Solana Explorer showing the split | "Anyone can release it, but only to the heirs on record. Every lamport and token is split by the on-chain shares." |
| 2:50 | Pulse dial | "Vigil. Clock in." |
