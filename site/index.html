Proof-of-life vault

Crypto has no next of kin.

Clock in once a day with a hold and a fingerprint. If you ever go quiet, your SOL, SKR and tokens go home to the people you chose.

How it works

Three steps. One habit.

01

Light it

Choose up to five heirs with shares, a rhythm and a grace period. One wallet approval creates the vault and deposits what you want protected.

02

Clock in

Hold, touch the sensor. A key sealed in your phone's hardware keystore signs a tiny pulse on Solana. No wallet pop-up.

03

If you go quiet

After your window and grace period, anyone can trigger the release. The program splits every lamport and token by the on-chain shares.
